# Superbuy Research Brief (US Buyer, 2026)

A practical research brief for a US-based buyer in 2026 planning to use Superbuy (superbuy.com) as a shopping agent for Taobao, Weidian, and 1688, with consolidated international shipping to the United States.

> Confidence note: Numbers in this brief are cross-referenced from Superbuy's own fee/help pages, JadeShip's agent comparison, HowToTao, the Superbuy "Fees Explained" community guide, JadeShip's customs guide, and discussion summaries from r/FashionReps / Trustpilot. Some details (especially shipping line availability and tariff handling) are changing rapidly in 2025-2026 due to the US ending the de minimis exemption — those items are flagged inline.

---

## 1. What Superbuy Is

Superbuy (founded 2012, operated by 360buy/independent in Shenzhen) is one of the oldest and largest "China shopping agent / parcel forwarder" services. The model:

1. You give Superbuy a product link (or have them source for you).
2. Superbuy buys the product **on your behalf** in China, paying the seller in RMB from your prepaid wallet.
3. The seller ships domestically to a Superbuy warehouse (Huizhou, Guangdong; sometimes Hong Kong).
4. Superbuy receives, inspects, and photographs the item. You QC.
5. You consolidate any number of items into one international parcel and pick a shipping line to the US.

Superbuy is one of the post-Pandabuy "big four/five" Western-facing agents alongside CSSBuy, Sugargoo, Hoobuy, and Kakobuy. It is often pitched as the **"reliable, mature, slightly more expensive"** option — broad marketplace coverage, long history, organized warehouse processes, but slower turnaround and pricier shipping than budget rivals.

## 2. End-to-End Flow

### 2.1 Account setup
- Sign up at superbuy.com (English UI at `/en/`).
- New user shipping coupons are common (commonly cited: ¥560 in coupons, sometimes an "$80 shipping coupon" promo). Verify on signup; values change.
- Top up your wallet — most US buyers use **Wise, VISA/Mastercard, PayPal, or Alipay**. Bitcoin is mentioned by some third-party comparisons. Top-up is the moment FX happens; expect ~5% effective spread vs spot RMB/USD when paying by card (per HowToTao). Wise typically gives the best rate.

### 2.2 Submitting a purchase
You have two main flows:

**A. Self-service (the URL flow you're already familiar with):**
- Paste a Taobao / Tmall / JD / 1688 / Weidian / Xianyu / Yupoo URL into the search bar, or hit the URL directly:
  `https://www.superbuy.com/en/page/buy/?url=<URL-encoded product link>`
- The page parses SKU options (size/color), you pick variant + quantity, then "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now."
- If the link parser fails (common for some Weidian/Xianyu/Yupoo links and any Telegram/seller-image link), use **DIY Order**: paste the link plus manual notes (size, color, price) and Superbuy's purchasing team places the order on your behalf.

**B. Expert Sourcing Service (a.k.a. "shipping expert" or "重购"):**
- For when you only have a photo, screenshot, or you want them to find a better price/seller. Superbuy buyers do the legwork.
- Fee: **10%** of order value, **min ¥20, max ¥300** (per Superbuy's fee page; was adjusted via "Notice of Expert Sourcing Service Fee Adjustment" in April 2025).

### 2.3 Purchase → warehouse
- After payment, Superbuy places the order with the seller. Status flows: **Purchasing → Purchased → Awaiting arrival → In warehouse**.
- Domestic Chinese shipping is paid by Superbuy on your behalf and **passed through at cost** (no markup, per their fee page).
- Stated SLA: **~6h to place an order, ~24h for QC photos after arrival, ~48h to dispatch a submitted parcel** (JadeShip). Real-world reports vary, especially around Chinese New Year and Singles' Day — Trustpilot reviews from late 2025 cite multi-week stalls during CNY 2025.

### 2.4 QC (Quality Check)
- Superbuy posts free basic QC photos on each item once it arrives in warehouse. Per JadeShip's comparison: typically **6 HD images** per item (some sources still say 3 SD — the spec was upgraded; expect 5–6 images covering main angles, defects, tags).
- If you want more, value-added options:
  - **Detailed photo / extra angle:** ¥2 per item
  - **Re-inspection:** ¥6 per item
  - **Model try-on photos:** ¥30 per item
  - **Product video:** ¥20 per item
  - **Power-on test (electronics):** ¥12 per item

You QC inside "My Warehouse." If you want to return, you click reject; refund/exchange is handled by the seller, not Superbuy — return policy is *up to the seller*, and you pay the domestic shipping back. (CSSBuy and Kakobuy have stricter agent-side return guarantees; this is a documented Superbuy weakness.)

### 2.5 Consolidation, packaging, value-added services
Once items are accepted, you "Submit Parcel" — pick the items in your warehouse to ship together. Before submission you set packaging options:

- **Free:** Remove outer packing box, remove product packaging box, remove inner packing bag, remove tags (request in remarks).
- **¥2/item:** Switching packaging (consolidate into smaller box).
- **¥4/item:** EPE (foam) packaging
- **¥5/item:** Folding shoebox (replaces full shoebox to save volume).
- **¥17/item:** Vacuum compression bag — max bag 100×80 cm. Big volumetric savings on soft goods (jackets, hoodies). Some users report mixed results ("vacuum was meh") — fabric springs back partially in transit.
- **¥20/parcel:** Rehearsal packaging (Superbuy mocks the parcel and gives you the actual dim-weight before you commit).
- **¥23/item:** Customized EPE
- **¥250/item:** Wooden case (fragile / high-value)
- **¥20/item:** Ironing
- **¥5/item:** Priority stock-in (3h SLA), ¥10/item priority stock-out (3h SLA).

### 2.6 International shipping
You pick a shipping line in the parcel submission flow. Superbuy advertises **~99 lines** in its shipping calculator (the most granular set of any major agent). Common categories:

- **Express courier:** DHL, FedEx, UPS — fastest (3–7 days to US), highest tariff exposure post-2025.
- **EMS / China Post (USPS hand-off):** historically the favored "stealth" line for US replicas (5–15 days, lower seizure rate per JadeShip's customs guide). **As of May 14, 2025, Superbuy paused most EMS-USPS routes to the USA** in response to tariff changes; status is fluid — check before you submit.
- **Yanwen, 4PX, etc. (economy/dedicated lines):** 15–40 days, cheaper. "Economy dedicated" lines undercut postal by 20–30%.
- **UPS Economy Hong Kong (Tax-Free)** and similar HK-origin routes — Superbuy gives an example of ¥428 for 1000g.

Superbuy's shipping calculator (`/en/page/query/freight/`) is widely regarded as the most detailed of any agent — line-by-line price + transit. Use **JadeShip's combined calculator** to compare Superbuy's line price against Sugargoo/CSSBuy/Kakobuy for the same weight + destination.

After shipment, tracking is via 17Track. Superbuy's initial shipping charge often **overestimates** real volumetric weight; refunds of the overage are issued to your wallet ~1–5 days post-dispatch (HowToTao).

## 4. 2026 State — What Changed Recently

This is the rapidly-changing part. As of May 2026 the relevant history:

- **April 2024:** Pandabuy collapsed. Chinese authorities raided warehouses; balances were frozen; Pandabuy never recovered as a serious agent. Mass migration to Superbuy / CSSBuy / Sugargoo / Hoobuy / Kakobuy / Mulebuy.
- **May 2, 2025:** US ended de minimis exemption *for China and Hong Kong* origin shipments — every Superbuy parcel from those origins to the US now technically owes duties.
- **May 14, 2025:** Superbuy paused most EMS-to-USA routes (USPS hand-off broken).
- **May 15, 2025:** Superbuy began collecting a **45%-of-declared-value duty deposit** on FedEx/UPS/DHL parcels to the US. The deposit is reconciled with actuals; over-collection is refunded.
- **August 29, 2025:** US ended de minimis *globally*. All parcels under $800 are now in scope for duties regardless of origin.
- **Until February 28, 2026:** Postal shipments could use a flat per-package duty ($80–$200) instead of ad valorem. **After Feb 28, 2026 all imports moved to tariff-based duties** — so right now, in May 2026, every Superbuy parcel pays full duties on declared value.
- **April 2025:** Superbuy adjusted Expert Sourcing Service fee structure (the 10%/min¥20/max¥300 cited above).

Practical 2026 implication for a US buyer: there is **no longer a "free" sub-$800 path**. Budget for ~10–25%+ duties on the declared value plus the carrier's brokerage. Choose the declared value honestly enough to avoid CBP holds — see §7.

## 6. The URL Flow (Practical)

The URL form `https://www.superbuy.com/en/page/buy/?url=<encoded-link>` is a deep-link to Superbuy's order page with the source URL pre-filled. It works for:

- `item.taobao.com/item.htm?id=...` and `detail.tmall.com/item.htm?id=...`
- `weidian.com/item.html?itemID=...` (sometimes; Weidian has multiple URL formats)
- `detail.1688.com/offer/...html`
- `m.tb.cn/...` short links (Superbuy parses these)

If the parser shows "failed to retrieve" or wrong SKU options:
1. Try expanding short URLs (Taobao "h5.m.taobao.com" or "tb.cn") to canonical desktop form first — JadeShip's universal link converter does this.
2. Fall back to the **DIY Order** form (manual SKU/price input).
3. Last resort, expert sourcing (10% fee).

A common "gotcha": the Taobao "tb.cn" share-format link sometimes opens but loses the SKU; copy the link from the desktop product page instead.

## 7. Tips & Pitfalls (Cross-Referenced from Reddit, Trustpilot, JadeShip)

**Do:**
- **Top up via Wise**, not credit card, to avoid the ~5% spread + card processing.
- **QC every item promptly** — sellers are far more cooperative on returns within ~7 days of arrival than after a month.
- **Always use a "rehearsal packaging" (¥20)** for first hauls — it tells you the real dim-weight before you commit; saves more than ¥20 if you'd otherwise pick the wrong service tier.
- **Remove all branded boxes / shoeboxes / poly bags** to cut volumetric weight. Free service.
- **Vacuum-bag soft goods** for big hauls (jackets, hoodies). ¥17/item, but can shrink a parcel 30–50%.
- **Split hauls under ~6–7 kg per parcel** to the US (per JadeShip's customs guide).
- **Declare reasonably** (under $130 was the pre-2025 rule of thumb). Post-de-minimis, declaration still matters because higher declared values invite CBP attention; truthful-but-conservative is safer than wildly low (which can trigger inspection).
- **Use JadeShip's combined calculator** before each shipment to sanity-check Superbuy's quote against Sugargoo/CSSBuy.
- **Save coupon codes** — Superbuy publishes shipping coupons for new users (~¥560 historically) and seasonal 10% shipping promos in their BBS/forum.

**Don't:**
- **Don't ship near Chinese New Year** (late Jan / early Feb) — warehouses slow to a crawl; multi-week stalls reported on Trustpilot 2025.
- **Don't sit on items past 90 days** — ¥0.1/item/day adds up fast on a 30-piece haul, and at 180 days the parcel can be auctioned/disposed.
- **Don't ship perfume, lighters, batteries, liquids, knives, food** — explicitly prohibited; near-certain seizure or refusal.
- **Don't put designer-logo replicas in the same parcel as obvious "loud" replicas** (Gucci/LV with Supreme/AMK) — JadeShip suggests separating risk profiles.
- **Don't trust Superbuy's first shipping estimate as final** — actual dim-weight reconciles after dispatch. Refunds come back to your wallet.
- **Don't expect agent-side returns** — Superbuy escalates to the seller; if the seller refuses, you eat it. CSSBuy/Kakobuy have stricter own-side guarantees here.

**Real complaints from late-2025 reviews:**
- CNY 2025 caused multi-week warehouse stalls with poor proactive comms.
- Some users hit with extra delivery cost requests after final invoice was paid (most often when the carrier reweighs).
- US-route disruption in May 2025 caught buyers off guard — submitted parcels stuck pending re-routing.

## 8. Bottom Line for a US Buyer in 2026

Superbuy is a **strong default choice** if your priorities are: marketplace coverage (Taobao + Weidian + 1688 in one platform), no service fee on Taobao, the most granular shipping-line selection in the industry, and a mature support org. It is **not the cheapest** for shipping (Sugargoo/CSSBuy beat it on lines), **not the fastest** (Kakobuy beats it on SLA), and **its returns are weak** vs newer rivals.

For a 2026 US workflow:
1. Sign up + grab new-user coupons.
2. Top up via Wise.
3. Use the `?url=` deep link or the URL bar for Taobao/Tmall/1688/Weidian items; fall back to DIY for stubborn links; expert sourcing only when you don't have a link.
4. QC within a week, request extra photos at ¥2 if the basic 6 don't show what you need.
5. Consolidate, vacuum-bag soft goods, remove all retail packaging.
6. Use the shipping calculator + cross-check on JadeShip; expect to pay duties (de minimis is dead).
7. Pick a non-EMS line (current EMS-USA pause); UPS Economy HK is a popular "tax-friendlier" option but still hit by the 45% deposit on FedEx/UPS/DHL routes.
8. Reconcile the dim-weight refund 1–5 days after dispatch.
